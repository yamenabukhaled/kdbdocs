# Q3 regional sales summary (draft)

Overall revenue grew 6% quarter over quarter, driven by the north region.
The south region declined 2% after a supplier delay in August.
Online orders now make up 41% of volume, up from 35% in Q2.

Recommendations
1. Prioritise restocking in the south before the holiday period.
2. Expand the online promotion that performed well in September.
