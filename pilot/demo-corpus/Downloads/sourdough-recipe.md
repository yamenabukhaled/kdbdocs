# Weekend sourdough

Starter: feed the night before, use when bubbly and doubled.

- 500 g bread flour
- 350 g water
- 100 g active starter
- 10 g salt

Mix flour and water, rest 45 minutes (autolyse). Add starter and salt.
Four sets of stretch and folds, 30 minutes apart. Bulk ferment until risen about 50%.
Shape, cold-proof overnight in the fridge. Bake at 250 C in a Dutch oven: 20 minutes lid on, 20 off.
