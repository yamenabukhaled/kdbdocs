# A short survey of spectral graph theory

Abstract. We review how eigenvalues of the graph Laplacian relate to connectivity, expansion and clustering.
The second-smallest eigenvalue (the Fiedler value) bounds how well a graph can be cut into two parts.
Cheeger's inequality links this eigenvalue to the graph's conductance.
Applications include spectral clustering, image segmentation and the analysis of random walks.
