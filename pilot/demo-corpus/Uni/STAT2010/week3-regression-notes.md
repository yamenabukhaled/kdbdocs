# STAT2010 Week 3 — Simple linear regression

Lecture notes, Tuesday.

- Model: y = b0 + b1 x + e, errors assumed independent with constant variance.
- Least squares chooses b0, b1 to minimise the sum of squared residuals.
- b1 = Sxy / Sxx. Interpretation: expected change in y for a one-unit change in x.
- R squared is the share of variance explained; it never decreases when adding predictors.
- Residual plots: look for funnels (heteroscedasticity) and curves (missing terms).
