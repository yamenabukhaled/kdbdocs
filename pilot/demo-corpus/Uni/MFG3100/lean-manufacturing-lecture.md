# MFG3100 Lecture 5 — Lean manufacturing

Seven wastes (TIMWOOD): transport, inventory, motion, waiting, overproduction, over-processing, defects.

Value stream mapping: draw current state, identify non-value-added time, design a future state.
Takt time = available production time / customer demand.
Kanban limits work in progress; pull rather than push.

Case study for next week's seminar: a bicycle frame plant reducing changeover time with SMED.
